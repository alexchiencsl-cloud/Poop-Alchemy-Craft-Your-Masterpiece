# Poop-Alchemy-Craft-Your-Masterpiece
Poop Alchemy is a hilarious stop-motion claymation style mobile web game where your diet decides your destiny! Combine up to 3 foods and 1 drink from 72 outrageous ingredients, nail the pressure gauge on the toilet, and discover 12 unique clay poop figurines. Can you unlock the legendary 24K Golden Banana Turd and score a perfect 100?
